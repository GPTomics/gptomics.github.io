Genomic Relativity

Series co-authors: Jason Chin and Domen Jemec

Genomic relativity is a developing way to describe genomes without treating any one reference assembly as the biological center. A genome is the physical nucleic acid itself, while a genome assembly is a representation reconstructed from sequencing reads. We are exploring how to describe genomic positions and variants without making them products of a single reference.

Essays

These essays begin with the practical consequences in clinical genome reports, then examine the deeper assumption that genomic meaning needs a privileged coordinate system at all. As we develop the theory, more essays will come.
